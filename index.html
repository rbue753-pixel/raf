Problem-Solving Guides & Heuristics

George Polya's 4-Step Strategy:

Step 1

Understand the Problem: Identify given values, unknowns, and basic conditions.

Step 2

Devise a Plan: Select the correct standard formula or theorem.

Step 3

Carry Out the Plan: Execute mathematical calculations accurately.

Step 4

Look Back: Check computation steps, verify units, and validate accuracy.

Non-Routine Heuristic Strategies:

Heuristic 1

Draw a Diagram / Visualize: Sketch real-life spatial relationships (e.g., Tabaco Port vectors or church rooftops).

Heuristic 2

Look for a Pattern: Examine sequence changes, tiling arrangements, or repetitive structures.

Heuristic 3

Work Backwards: Trace from target real-world goal state back to initial conditions.

Heuristic 4

Solve a Simpler Sub-Problem: Deconstruct complex real-world dimensions into manageable parts.

Parts of Geometry for BSED Math Majors


  • Axiomatic Systems & Incidence Geometry: Defined/undefined terms, postulates, and deductive frameworks.
  • Euclidean & Non-Euclidean Geometry: Parallel postulates, spherical, and hyperbolic geometries.
  • Transformation Geometry: Rigid motions (translations, reflections, rotations) and similarity transformations.
  • Coordinate & Analytical Geometry: Vector spaces, conic sections, and algebraic geometry proofs.
  • Rigorous Proof Techniques: Direct proofs, proof by contradiction, and geometric constructions.

Contextualized Example (Tabaco Albay)

Scenario: A passenger ferry leaves Tabaco City Port heading North for 12 km while a fishing vessel heads East for 5 km. What is the direct distance between them?

Solution (Pythagorean Theorem):
Distance² = 12² + 5² = 144 + 25 = 169.
Distance = √169 = 13 kilometers.
